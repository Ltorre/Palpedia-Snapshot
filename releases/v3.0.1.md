# Palpedia Snapshot v3.0.1

v3.0.1 improves the breeding planner result display. After selecting a real male and female Pal, the exact child is now shown in a prominent purple-outlined result card, matching the target treatment in the quickest-route family tree. The card keeps the breeding rule and generic target rank directly below the child name.

## Download and use

1. Download `palpedia-snapshot-windows-amd64.exe` from this release and open it normally.
2. Select a `Level.sav` from your Palworld save. Steam local worlds are normally under `C:\Users\<WindowsUser>\AppData\Local\Pal\Saved\SaveGames\<SteamID>\<WorldID>\Level.sav`.
3. Choose **Breeding planner**, select one male and one female Pal, then click **Calculate selected pair**. Read the result in the new outlined **Exact child** card.

The app remains read-only: it never modifies the selected save or its directory, and makes no network requests at runtime. The exact child calculation uses the bundled breeding-rule snapshot; passive inheritance and egg sex remain subject to Palworld RNG.
